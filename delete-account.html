Delete your Nept Track account

Nept Track: Habit & Money, by Demo Station

In the app

  1. Open Nept Track and go to Settings.
  2. Open Premium (your account).
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Your account is deleted straight away. This cannot be undone.

If you cannot open the app

Email demostationplay@gmail.com from the email address of your account, with the subject "Delete my Nept Track account". We delete the account within 30 days and reply when it is done.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in account and email address.
  • Your profile: name, photo, birthday, country, address, hobby and what you do.
  • Your cloud backups.
  • Your invite code, points and invite history.
  • Your account activity (last used, app version).

What is not deleted

  • The data on your phone. Tasks, money records and notes stay in the app. To remove them too, use Settings → Erase all data, or uninstall the app.
  • A paid subscription. Deleting the account does not cancel it. Cancel it in Google Play → Payments and subscriptions.
  • Purchase records. Google Play and RevenueCat keep the record of a purchase, linked to a random app ID and not to your name or email, for as long as tax and accounting law requires.

To delete only some data and keep the account: clear the details in your profile in the app. Cloud backups are removed when the account is deleted; we keep only your five most recent.
